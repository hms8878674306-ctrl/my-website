Forgive Me

I wrote this because some things are too important to say in a text. This is everything I feel — no filters, no excuses.

When you're ready, open my letter.

My dearest love,

I don't know where to begin, except to say the two words that matter most: I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for the pain I caused you. I'm sorry for the moments I made you feel unloved, when loving you is the only thing that has ever made sense to me. You deserved better, and I hate that I wasn't better for you.

Ever since we started talking on 20 April, you've become my favourite notification, my favourite person, my favourite everything. Losing you — or even the thought of you being hurt because of me — is the scariest thing I've ever faced.

I love you. I love you. I love you.

Not as a habit. Not as a comfort. I love you in the way the moon loves the tide — constantly, quietly, and without ever stopping. I love your strength, your softness, your fire, your silence. I love all of you, even the parts you think are hard to love.

I won't ask you to forgive me right now if you're not ready. But I will spend every day showing you that my love is real, my regret is deep, and my commitment to us is unbreakable.

If you give me one more chance, I swear on everything I am — I will spend the rest of my life making sure you never doubt my love again.

Forever yours,
with all my love and all my heart.
